THE PRACTICE

ABOUT AESTHETE

AESTHETE is a multidisciplinary creative practice inspired by dark naturalism, ethereal beauty and haunting melancholy.

The work moves between entomology art, fine art, photography and creative portraiture, modeling, costume creation, digital art, illustration, music and more.

At its heart is a fascination with the beauty of life cycles, the natural world, and the stories held within the things we often overlook.
